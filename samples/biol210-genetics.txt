BIOL 210 - Genetics and Genomes
Sample University, Department of Biology
Winter 2027

Week-by-week schedule
Week 1: Mendelian genetics, Punnett squares, test crosses
Week 2: Chromosomes and linkage, recombination, gene mapping
Week 3: DNA as genetic material, replication, repair of DNA damage
Week 4: Gene expression: transcription, RNA processing, translation
Week 5: Regulation of gene expression in bacteria (the lac operon) and in eukaryotes
Week 6: Mutations and their effects. Reading week at the end of this week.
Week 7: Population genetics and Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium
Week 8: Genomics, sequencing technologies, and CRISPR gene editing
